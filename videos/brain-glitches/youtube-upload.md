The Doorway Effect: why your brain erases a thought the instant you cross into another room.

The Hypnic Jerk: the ancient alarm that throws your body awake as you fall asleep.
The Earworm: the unfinished loop your brain replays for days, chasing an ending that never comes.
Déjà Vu: the filing error that lets you remember a moment as it happens.

Here's the part almost nobody says out loud: none of these are your brain breaking. They are the price of a brain fast enough to keep you alive.
