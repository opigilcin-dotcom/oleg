## 3. Recommended pairing & A/B test

**Primary recommendation:** Title #1 **"Why You Forget Why You Walked Into a Room"** + Thumbnail **A (WHY YOU FORGET? / giant red ?)**.
- **Why:** The thumbnail text ("WHY YOU FORGET?") and the title don't duplicate — the thumb poses the raw question, the title adds the twist ("…why you walked into a room"), widening the curiosity gap instead of repeating it. The doorway visual + red "?" reads instantly at phone size and signals the topic without jargon.

**A/B test plan:**
- **Test 1 (thumbnail):** A (WHY YOU FORGET?) vs C (WHY AM I HERE?) against the same #1 title. A = topic clarity; C = raw relatability/humor. See which emotional angle wins clicks.

- Pair Thumbnail B (THE DOORWAY EFFECT) only with the term-led title/RU #3 if you want the search/SEO play, since both name the concept.
