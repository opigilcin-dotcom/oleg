**Озвучка (verbatim из script.md):** "In 2011, a psychologist named Gabriel Radvansky ran a study at the University of Notre Dame. People moved through rooms on a screen, picking up an object on one table, then carrying it and putting it down on another. Sometimes they walked the exact same distance, but through a doorway. The distance was identical. Only the doorway changed. After walking through a doorway, people forgot what they were carrying far more often. The only thing that hurt their memory was passing through the door."
