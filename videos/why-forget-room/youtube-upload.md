You stand up with a clear reason, walk through a doorway — and it's gone. Why?

It's called the Doorway Effect. Your brain slices your day into separate "events," and a doorway is one of the strongest boundaries there is. The moment you cross it, your brain clears the desk for the next event — and the reason you got up slips through the gap.

In this video: the 2011 Notre Dame experiment, why working memory only holds about 4 things, the scuba-diver study, and the simple trick that actually brings the memory back.
