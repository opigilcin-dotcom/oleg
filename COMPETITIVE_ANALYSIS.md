## Common DNA — all winners share these 6
1. **"Forbidden/forgotten" framing** in title (They Don't Want You to Find / No One Can Explain).
2. **A number** — a count (10/12/17 places) OR a time gap (60 Years Later).
3. **Cold open, zero intro** — first sentence is the hook.
4. **Open loop in first 15s** — "Stick around for number one."
5. **Faceless, batch-produced, 20-27 min** long-form (mid-roll ad sweet spot).
6. **Geo/identity anchoring for comment bait** — a state, county, or anonymized victim.

## ✅ COPY 1:1 (8 proven tactics)
1. Forbidden clause, rotated: swap bank of "They Don't Want You to Find / You're Not Supposed to Know / NEVER Meant to Find / No One Can Explain".
2. Default title: `[N] [GEO] [Subject] [forbidden clause]!!`
3. One video per state/region — content calendar as a geographic checklist.
4. 20-25 min, 10-13 item countdown.
5. "Praise → but → exclusivity" cold open (lift verbatim shape).
6. Pin the #1 tease in first 12s.
7. Numbered list + "Comment where you're from" in description.
8. DNA variant for RPM: `[Anonymized victim] + [N Years Later] + DNA Revealed [Why]`.

## 🎯 MAKE UNIQUE (5 edges — where they're weak)
1. **Brand identity** — niche is visually interchangeable (5/6 thumbnail neighbors are clones). Consistent colors + logo + recurring narrator persona stands out instantly.
2. **Mid-video retention/accuracy** — add one genuinely surprising, sourced fact + map/archival photo per item to beat the post-item-3 drop-off.
3. **Topic-ceiling hedge** — hybrid "forensic history of a forgotten place" (DNA RPM + abandoned-places runway). No benchmark owns this overlap.
4. **Format variety** — add a recurring 8-12 min high-VPH "reveal" short (Browser Explained style) alongside the 22-min flagship.
5. **Premium hook audio** — borrow Othram's multi-voice/witness cold open (even synthetic voices) to beat the flat-AI-VO pack.
