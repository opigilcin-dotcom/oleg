## Title formula
- `How [surprising outcome] From [ordinary thing]`
- `Why [place/group] [surprising thing], and [contrast]`
- `Why [thing] Actually [counter-intuitive]` — the "actually/ordinary/impossible/misunderstood" lever.

## Hook (proven)
Problem → obvious-but-wrong assumption → "But [someone] found an unusual solution." No intro. Fast cuts every 4-8s with one-word stat/location overlays (ATACAMA, 15.000L, 100KM/H).

## ⚠️ Visual production = HYBRID (key finding)
- ~55% **licensed real stock footage** (dunes, factory lines, water) + ~45% **AI** (named people, dramatized storms, conceptual scenes).
- **NOT 100% AI-replicable as-is** — the real-footage b-roll is the dependency.

## Production-ease verdict (vs pure-AI ghost towns = 1.5/5)
- Vodar as-is = **3/5**: visual sourcing 3/5 (needs real stock), research 4/5 (facts must be accurate), topic supply 1/5 (infinite).

## ✅ BEST SUB-ANGLE: "What's at the bottom of X / deep ocean & extreme-Earth phenomena"
Wins on BOTH of the user's axes (simpler + more beautiful):
1. **Fully AI-producible** — almost no real footage exists of the Mariana Trench floor / volcano interiors, so AI is the only way (no licensed-footage dependency → ease drops to ~1.5/5).
2. **Most visually stunning** — bioluminescent abyss, trench creatures, magma, auroras render gorgeously and are thumbnail-friendly.
3. **Inherits Vodar's title formula** unchanged: "What's Really at the Bottom of the Mariana Trench", "Why [creatures] Actually Live in the Abyss".
4. **Lower research risk** than refinery engineering.

Runner-up: natural phenomena / Earth wonders (auroras, geysers, ice caves) — same pure-AI, high-beauty profile.

**Bottom line:** clone Vodar's title + hook + fast-cut/overlay structure, migrate the topic axis to deep-ocean / extreme-Earth / cosmic phenomena → pure-AI pipeline at ghost-towns ease + Vodar's proven 200-500K-view curiosity engine. Avoid: bankrupt-startups / yacht-economics (abstract = low views, hard to make beautiful).
