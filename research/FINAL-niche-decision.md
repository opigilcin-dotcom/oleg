## WINNER: SPACE / COSMOS
**Build a faceless, fully-AI space/cosmos documentary channel.**

Why:
1. Perfect 5 on our #1 axis — pure-AI: nebulae, planets, black holes have no real reference to mismatch, no human anatomy to botch. AI renders space better than almost anything.
2. Best RPM of feasible niches — multiple channels at the **$7.68** ceiling (Acronium, Free Documentary-Space, BRIGHT SIDE SPACE NEWS).
3. Widest-open window: **Cosmicus = $12,642/mo at <5 months old.** BRIGHT SIDE SPACE NEWS profitable at 6mo.
4. Infinite runway, low factual risk (awe-driven, loosely sourced). NexLev tracks a dedicated 233-channel space category.

Proven channels: Cosmicus (4.8mo, 89.8K, $12,642, RPM 5.04) · Free Documentary-Space ($7,566, RPM 7.68) · Acronium (11mo, $3,588, RPM 7.68) · BRIGHT SIDE SPACE NEWS (6mo, $2,316, RPM 7.68).

Economics (faceless AI, 2-3 uploads/wk): Month 3 ~$300-1,500 · Month 6 ~$2-4K · Month 12 ~$5-12K.

Format: 10-20 min AI docs. AI stills + image-to-video pans over nebulae, single emotive AI TTS, cinematic ambient bed. ONE awe/fear hook per video ("This planet rains glass sideways at 5,400 mph"). Curiosity/dread thumbnails — genre lives on CTR.
